This is the one question everything else is read for. The answer is assembled from independent
measurements, not from advertising.

## The professionals' benchmark

The best crypto-arbitrage funds with over $100M under management aim for **15–25% net a year** — and
often fall short of even that. That is the industry's ceiling: dedicated teams, institutional
infrastructure, direct exchange channels. Retail works below that ceiling, not above it.

## What "120% a year" actually requires

To turn $1 into $2.20 in a year you need only **0.216% net a day** with daily reinvestment — about 6.8%
a month. It sounds modest until you compare it with what each strategy gives on its own.

## Returns by strategy

| Strategy | What measurements show |
|---|---|
| Cross-exchange spot (BTC, ETH) | spreads 0.05–0.3%; after fees and withdrawal, **0.01–0.1%** per trade remains |
| Triangular | a peer-reviewed 2025 study examined 4,879 opportunities — **none** stayed profitable after fees |
| Funding rate | around **19% a year** on average (2025); between exchanges, up to ~33% |
| Cash-and-carry (spot + futures) | **6–10% a year**, up to 20–25% in euphoria |

## The takeaway

An honest retail benchmark is single-digit to low-double-digit percent a YEAR, not a day. Anything
promising more is either counting gross instead of net, or swapping a year for a month, or simply
selling a dream.
