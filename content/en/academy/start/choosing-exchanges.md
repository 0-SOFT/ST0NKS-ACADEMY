There is no single answer: some advise holding a dozen-plus venues, an independent study caps the number at five to seven. Let us look at what it actually depends on.

## How many exchanges

| Number | Assessment |
|---|---|
| under 3 | too few arbitrage pairs |
| 5–7 | the optimum: a balance of opportunity and capital fragmentation |
| over 8 | capital spread too thin, operational complexity grows |

It is also sensible to keep 10–15% of the portfolio in stablecoins as a buffer for rebalancing and urgent withdrawals.

## The limit that decides for you

Before choosing on quality, count the money. A sensible ceiling is no more than 20–30% of capital on one exchange.

| Capital | Per exchange across 5 | Workable |
|---|---|---|
| $1,000 | $200 | no: fees eat the position |
| $10,000 | $2,000 | just barely |
| $50,000 | $10,000 | yes |

The takeaway: on small capital, opening five exchanges is pointless — the money fragments before the first pair appears. Start with two or three and expand as the account grows.

## Two different questions

Choosing a venue is really two questions that are often confused.

- **Where to hold money.** The criterion is regulatory status and proof of reserves. An exchange failure can lock capital away for good.
- **Where pairs appear.** The criterion is coverage and liquidity. A venue can be convenient for trades yet unfit as a vault.

## Baskets of exchanges

Venues differ in how they compute the funding rate, and that determines which side of a pair you open on them: on some a long is better, on others a short. The practical upshot: you need exchanges from both groups. A set from just one gives pairs but not the right ones.

## The order of choosing

1. Count how many exchanges the capital can bear.
2. Take venues from both baskets.
3. Check where you keep the main balance — by reliability, not by pair count.
4. Exclude what cannot be computed: an exchange that changes its rate in response to your own trade is unfit.
5. Account for limits on fresh listings — the spread is visible but there is nothing to enter with.
6. Do not exceed 20–30% of capital on one venue.

## About exchange addresses

Never type an exchange address from memory: one wrong character leads to a phishing site, and the cost of the error is the whole deposit. Follow only a verified link and check the address in the browser bar.
