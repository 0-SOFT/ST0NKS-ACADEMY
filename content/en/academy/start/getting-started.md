Crypto arbitrage is earning on the price difference of the same asset across venues. If a coin is cheaper on one exchange and dearer on another, you buy where it is cheaper and sell where it is dearer. The task comes down to two actions: find the divergence and act on it in time.

## Be careful about how easy it sounds

Arbitrage is often described as "simple, accessible even to a beginner". Independent measurements say otherwise: professional funds with over $100M of capital aim for 15–25% a year and often fall short. Before counting profit, read the economics section.

## Why prices diverge

**Liquidity** is the ability to buy or sell an asset without noticeably moving the price. Where few people trade, the price moves more easily and drifts from the market.

**Update lag.** Exchanges do not refresh quotes at the same instant. While one venue has already moved, another still holds the old price — that gap is the opportunity.

Hence an important warning: a price gap is not always an opportunity. Sometimes it means something is wrong with the asset or the exchange — see the risks section.

## Five kinds

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| Cross-exchange | Buy cheaper on one exchange, sell dearer on another |
| Intra-exchange | A gap between pairs, or between spot and futures on one exchange |
| Futures | A price gap between futures on different exchanges |
| Cross-network | One token priced differently across blockchain networks |
| Funding | Earning on the funding-rate difference; the asset's own price does not matter |

The mechanics of each are covered in the arbitrage-types section.

## Three beginner mistakes

1. **Fees not counted.** The gap is visible at once, the fees are not. There are four: trading (0.1–1%), deposit and withdrawal, transfers between exchanges, and bridges between networks. Count them all before the trade, not after.
2. **Slow transactions.** While funds travel between exchanges, the price moves. Choose fast networks and check their load before sending.
3. **Too little liquidity.** The price looks good but you cannot sell into it — there are no orders on the other side. Read the order book and gauge depth before entering.
