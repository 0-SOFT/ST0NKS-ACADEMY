**KYC** (Know Your Customer) is verification of a user's identity. Regulators are tightening anti-money-laundering controls and exchanges follow suit: anonymous trading on large venues is over. Without KYC an exchange's functionality is heavily limited.

## Verification tiers

There are usually several tiers, each lifting some restrictions.

| | Basic (no KYC) | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| How to get | automatic after email confirmation | ID document and selfie | Tier 1 plus proof of address |
| Crypto deposit | usually forbidden | a daily limit | higher limit |
| Fiat deposit and P2P | no | yes | yes |
| Withdrawal | tightly limited | high limit | higher still |

The exact limits differ by venue; what matters is the structure: the higher the tier, the fewer the restrictions.

## The basic-tier trap

Formally some operations work without KYC, but that is deceptive. On several venues, assets obtained without verification cannot be moved to the main account until KYC is passed. Trading looks available — yet you cannot take the money out. So it is wise to start verification at once: it takes from days to weeks.

## How Tier 1 is done

1. Find the prompt to verify — usually a banner on the exchange's pages, or in the account area.
2. Give your country of citizenship and document type.
3. Photograph the document and take a selfie looking into the camera. The image must be sharp and every detail legible. If there is no camera, upload scans manually.

The check usually takes from half an hour to an hour, up to a day under load.

## Tier 2

Tier 2 requires a document proving your residential address: a bank statement, a utility bill, or an address certificate. After the check the status changes and limits rise.
