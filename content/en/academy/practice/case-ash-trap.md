A huge spread growing before your eyes and generous funding look like the best opportunity of the day.
In fact it is a trap with no exit: the prices will not converge, because they have nothing to converge
to.

## Four signs that coincided

| Sign | What was seen |
|---|---|
| Mark price detached from the index | a multiple, not a percentage |
| Withdrawals and deposits closed | the coin physically cannot be moved |
| The spread only grows | it grows and grows, without stopping |
| Funding works against the position | and accrues the whole time you hold |

Each sign alone is a reason to be careful. Together they mean the pair is unsolvable in principle.

## Why the prices will not converge

A mark price converges to the index through trading on the venues the index is built from. If a coin's
withdrawal is closed, arbitrageurs cannot move the spot — so the index is never reached, and the spread
never closes. Funding, meanwhile, keeps eating the position.

## The takeaway

A giant spread with closed withdrawals is not an opportunity but a sign that there is nothing to
converge to. Check the withdrawal BEFORE entering, not after.
