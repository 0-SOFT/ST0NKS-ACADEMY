Four strategies often mentioned but rarely examined. For each: what it is and whether it is available to
retail.

| Strategy | Return | Retail |
|---|---|---|
| Triangular | 5–20% a year | formally yes, in practice doubtful |
| Cash-and-carry | depends on the basis | yes |
| Statistical | Sharpe 0.5–1.5 | yes — needs no low latency |
| Latency | — | no — tens of thousands invested plus a subscription |

**Triangular** — three pairs on one exchange (e.g. BTC→ETH→USDT→BTC). The fees of three trades eat the
gap: a peer-reviewed study found no profitable opportunity after costs.

**Cash-and-carry** — buy spot, sell futures, live on the basis; close to funding arbitrage. Available to
retail.

**Statistical** — trading on temporary divergences of correlated assets; needs no ultra-fast
infrastructure, so it is genuinely accessible.

**Latency** — earning on milliseconds ahead of everyone else; needs expensive infrastructure and is out
of reach for retail.

## The takeaway

What is genuinely available to retail is not what sounds more impressive but what needs no ultra-speed
and no huge capital. The prettier the name, the more carefully you look at the entry threshold.
